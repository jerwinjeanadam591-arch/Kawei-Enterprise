Hydropower Civil Infrastructure Project

Kawei Enterprises delivered civil construction support for hydro-power infrastructure, with work coordinated around approved drawings, site conditions and project safety requirements. The scope required disciplined supervision of earthworks, concrete structures and related installations.

The project demonstrates Kawei's practical capability in civil works, programme coordination, quality control and construction of durable infrastructure.
