Privacy Policy

Kawei Enterprises Co. Ltd respects the privacy of visitors who use this website. This policy explains how information submitted through our enquiry forms is handled.

Information we collect

We may receive your name, email address, telephone number, project details and message when you contact us by email, WhatsApp or a website form.

How information is used

Information is used only to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, discuss projects and provide service updates. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Website forms

Static website forms prepare a message in the visitor's email application. The visitor chooses whether to send it. No completed form is stored on this website unless a secure server integration is later configured.

Retention and sharing

Enquiry information is retained only as needed for business communication and project administration. It may be shared with relevant authorised employees or professional advisers when necessary to respond to an enquiry.

Security

Please avoid sending passwords, bank details or confidential documents through an initial enquiry. Use a secure approved channel for sensitive project information.
